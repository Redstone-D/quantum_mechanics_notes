\begin{dfn}[Unperturbed State]{unperturbed-state}
  For a Hamiltonian $\hat{H}$ where we have a known solution to the time independent Schrodinger equation, $\hat{H}\ket{\psi_n} = E_n\ket{\psi_n}$, the \emph{unperturbed state} is the state $\ket{\psi_n}$ with energy $E_n$. Such $\hat H$ is called the \emph{unperturbed Hamiltonian}. 
\end{dfn}
